What We Do

Roofs for Homes & Businesses

New Heights Roofing and Exteriors is a Little Rock roofing company for homes and businesses. We tear off and replace worn roofs, rebuild rotted decking, put down new flat roofs, work on metal roofs and seal up chimneys. After a storm, we help you through the insurance claim and cover your roof for free until the adjuster settles. Every roof quote is free.

Our Work

Recent Roofs We’ve Built

New shingle roofs, a flat roof, a storefront metal roof and a full tear-off down to the rafters. Real jobs from our own crew.

April 2023 · New Roof

A Brick Home’s New Roof

A two-story brick home got a complete new roof in dark architectural shingles. The homeowners picked their colour from CertainTeed Landmark and TAMKO sample boards, and we finished it with new pipe boots and roof vents. The first comment on the photos: “5 STAR for this kind of workmanship.”

  • Colour chosen from real shingle samples
  • New pipe boots and roof vents
  • Clean, straight ridge and hip lines

January 2020 · Commercial

A Storefront Metal Roof

A drive-thru store’s canopy roof, the first thing customers see from the road, was redone in burgundy metal roofing panels. It’s the kind of business roof we take on alongside our home jobs.

  • Burgundy metal panels on the canopy
  • Clean edges along the storefront
  • A commercial job beside our home roofs

January 2020 · Flat Roof

A Flat Roof Replacement

The old flat roof over this home was worn through and rotting underneath. We tore it off, replaced the bad decking, laid new underlayment and finished it with a brand-new flat roof that sheds water the way it should.

  • Old flat roof torn off
  • Rotted decking replaced
  • New underlayment and flat roof

September 2019 · Tear-Off

Down to the Rafters and Back

When we stripped this roof, the decking underneath had rotted through in places. We cut out the bad wood down to the rafters, put down new plywood and felt, and finished with tan architectural shingles and new turbine vents.

  • Full tear-off to the decking
  • Rotted wood cut out and replaced
  • New shingles and turbine vents
